I am a Senior Researcher in the Speech and Language Technology Lab (SLT) of the German Research Center for Artificial Intelligence (DFKI) in Berlin. I have a backgorund in Computer Science and Computational Linguistics, and work on topics related to natural language processing and AI applications, applied to medical use cases and healthcare. My research intersts include trustworthy clinical decision support, multimodal machine learning, LLM-based agents, and text anonymization. I am involved in a range of interdisciplinary projects across medical domains, including nephrology, emergency medicine, tumor boards, and sexual medicine. In addition, I am currently responsible for the basic and the advanced lecture for Natural Langauge Processing at TU Berlin.

If you are interessed to collaborate on research, or if you search for an intersting topic for your Master thesis, feel free to get in touch!

Here some recent highlights:

  • Co-organization of the ETHICAIA (Ethical Aspects of AI in Medicine) Workshop at IJCAI-ECAI 2026 in Bremen.

  • The findings of our clinical study of the PRIMA-AI project have been published at npj Digital Medicine.

  • DialogPII, our multilingual dialog anonymization dataset covering 11 languages, has been finally published.

  • Co-organization of the HeMAI (Multimodal Interaction with Generative AI Health Applications) workshop at ICMI 2026.

  • Senior Area Chair for ACL 2026.
